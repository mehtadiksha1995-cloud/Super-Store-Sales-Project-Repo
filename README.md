**Project Description**
Developed an end-to-end sales analytics project using advanced data analysis and time series forecasting techniques to uncover actionable business insights. Built interactive dashboards to monitor sales performance, analyze trends, and generate accurate sales forecasts, enabling data-driven decision-making and improved business growth.

**Key Highlights*
*Performed data cleaning, transformation, and analysis on retail sales data
Applied time series analysis for sales forecasting and trend identification
Built interactive dashboards for KPI tracking and business insights
Analyzed sales, profit, customer segments, and regional performance
Delivered actionable recommendations to support strategic decision-making
